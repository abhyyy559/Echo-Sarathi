## 1. Startup Details (Short Answer) — 178 chars

Building India's domain-independent AI voice-calling platform: agents that place real phone calls, converse naturally, and return structured data — starting with colleges tracking absent students.

## 2. Startup Innovation Overview — 172 chars

Domain-independent AI voice agents that make real outbound calls, converse naturally (<900ms response time, handles interruptions), and extract structured data. New use cases are config files — not new code.

## 3. Problem Statement — 238 chars

Institutions run on manual outbound calls — colleges spend days phoning absentees and logging notes by hand. Existing bots are robotic IVRs people hang up on; global AI callers aren't built for Indian telephony, languages or TRAI/DPDP rules.

## 4. Proposed Solution — 245 chars

Admins upload contacts and launch a campaign; our AI agent calls each person, holds a human-like conversation, extracts structured fields (reason, expected date), escalates uncertain answers to staff, and exports clean transcripts and reports.

## 5. What makes your solution unique? — 232 chars

Sub-second, barge-in-native conversations tuned for Indian phone audio — not scripted IVR. New call flows ship as config files, zero code. Compliance-by-design: DLT registration, consent records, AI disclosure, India data residency.

## 6. Target Customers — 230 chars

Colleges & universities first — same-day attendance, admission and fee follow-ups without staffing call teams. Then any structured-outreach workload: clinics (appointments), lenders (payment reminders), HR (screening), logistics (confirmations).

## 7. Market Opportunity — 222 chars

India's voice-AI adoption is accelerating, but managed vendors charge ₹4.5–9/min plus ₹1–6 lakh/month platform fees — pricing out institutions and SMEs. At ~₹3–5/min self-built cost, we unlock that massive underserved segment.

## 8. Business Model — 196 chars

B2B SaaS: monthly subscription tiered by concurrent-call capacity, plus transparent per-minute usage billing (~₹3–5/min). Paid setup for custom call domains; self-serve and developer-API tiers as we scale.

## 9. Go-to-Market Strategy — 233 chars

Win local colleges with free pilots proving same-day absentee coverage vs days of staff time; convert to annual contracts. Expand via measurable case studies and ERP/LMS + CPaaS partner channels, adding verticals through config-only onboarding.

## 10. Why do you require the GENESIS EIR Grant? — 218 chars

Our core pipeline is proven via live end-to-end test calls. The grant funds telephony minutes, STT/TTS APIs and DLT registration to reach paying college pilots, plus mentorship on DLT/DPDP compliance and full-time founder focus.
