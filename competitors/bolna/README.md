# Bolna — competitor profile

## Scope and evidence confidence
- Evidence date: 2026-10-28; supplied observations from the main agent's Browser Use session.
- Homepage and pricing page were opened; this rewrite adds no browser or web research.
- Review covered public text/DOM content and structure, not interactive or visual evaluation.
- Screenshots and clicks failed with `NATIVE_BROWSER_VIEWPORT_UNAVAILABLE` (0x0 unattached surface).
- No signup, demo execution, paid action, call, listening test, or logged-in use occurred.
- Confidence is high in the recorded public wording, not in independently verified delivery.
- Customer counts, language coverage and deployment capabilities remain vendor claims.

## Positioning and exact headline
- Exact headline: **“Voice AI Built for India”**.
- Positioning interpretation: an India-focused voice-agent builder with no-code and API paths.
- Industry templates make the prior description of an almost entirely generic builder inaccurate.
- This is a relevant direct comparator; no numerical threat or quality score is justified here.

## Publicly advertised features
- Inbound and outbound voice agents.
- Templates for BFSI, ecommerce, EdTech, healthcare and hospitality.
- Agent configuration from a template or from scratch.
- Click-to-call, campaigns and API-triggered calls.
- Human transfer and integrations with n8n, Make and Zapier.
- The feature section advertises 20+ languages; the headline language claim is 10+ Indic languages.
- These are separate published descriptions, not verified counts of supported language pairs.
- 20+ model choices and bring-your-own-key (BYOK) support.
- Region deployment options are advertised; no deployment was provisioned or verified.

## Public acquisition flow versus gated dashboard
- Published sequence: Connect Account -> Configure Agent -> Click to Call/campaign or APIs.
- Configure Agent offers the template-or-scratch choice in the public explanation.
- A $5 trial-credit offer provides a public entry point; credit delivery was not tested.
- This is a described acquisition/build path, not a completed onboarding walkthrough.
- Account requirements, checkout steps and time to first successful call remain unknown.
- Dashboard controls, campaign execution and report/export behavior were not inspected.

| Selected bundled models | $0.06/min; page also displays ₹5.52/min | STT, LLM and TTS bundled; telephony and platform fee extra |

| Smaller pilot pack | $300 / 6,500 minutes | Pilot-specific terms |
| Trial | $5 credits | Not converted into an assumed number of usable minutes |
| Enterprise | Customizable | Terms require confirmation |

- The displayed $0.06 and ₹5.52 imply ₹92/USD, not the prior profile's ₹88/USD assumption.
- That arithmetic describes the page display; it is not an exchange-rate or INR-settlement guarantee.
- 30-second billing pulses and a maximum of 25 concurrent calls apply to the pilot offer.
- Do not extend either pilot condition to all plans; enterprise capacity is customizable.
- The FAQ separately describes token-based pay-as-you-go pricing.
- BYOK is offered; this evidence does not establish a universal BYOK discount formula.
- No complete India call quote, tax treatment, GST-invoice or purchase-order policy was verified.

## Public frontend-stack signals
- Public asset paths indicate Next.js; Geist was observed as a font.
- These are public frontend signals, not proof of the account application or backend stack.
- No screenshot-based layout judgment, pointer behavior or runtime benchmark is available.

## UX lessons and hypotheses, not proven gaps
- Lesson: connect a country-specific promise to recognizable industry templates.
- Lesson: explain the no-code path and API path without conflating their prerequisites.
- Hypothesis: an institution-specific roster-to-outcome example could help buyers judge fit.
- Hypothesis: a quote separating models, platform, carrier and taxes could reduce budgeting uncertainty.
- Validate those ideas with users; they do not demonstrate that Bolna lacks corresponding features.
- Extraction, consent controls, tenant branding, INR settlement and procurement support remain unverified.
- No comparative voice quality, latency, onboarding speed or campaign success result was measured.

## Corrections from the prior profile
- Removed claims of no structured outcomes, no vertical templates and no tenant identity support.
- Removed unsupported assertions that GST invoices, INR billing and purchase orders are unavailable.
- Replaced the imagined signup/dashboard walkthrough with the publicly described sequence.
- Restricted the 25-call cap and 30-second pulses to the pilot rather than all plans.
- Removed rupee savings claims, trial-minute estimates and assumed cost-floor or margin comparisons.
- Replaced stale headline language and acknowledged the advertised industry-template coverage.
