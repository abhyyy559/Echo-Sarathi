# Acefone — competitor profile

Researched 2026-10-28. **Their site blocks automated access** (Cloudflare
"you have been blocked" in a headless browser, 403 on direct fetch). Pricing
below comes from **Capterra** and from **Acefone's own blog**, and is marked
accordingly.

**One-liner:** Cloud telephony and contact-centre for SMBs, now with AI voice
agents.
**Type:** SMB cloud telephony / contact centre (India + UK + US presence).
**Threat level:** **Medium.** Seat pricing makes them expensive for a small
office, which is our buyer.

---

## 1. What they sell

Cloud phone system for small teams: virtual numbers, IVR, call routing,
call recording, outbound dialer, contact-centre features, and an AI voice agent
line. Marketed on ease of setup and support rather than enterprise scale.

## 2. Feature set

| Area | What they ship |
|---|---|
| Telephony | Virtual numbers (local + toll-free), IVR, call groups |
| Contact centre | Queue, routing, live dashboard, recordings, monitoring |
| Outbound | Dialer/campaign calling, click-to-call |
| AI | AI voice agents (conversational), AI-assisted agent features |
| Integrations | CRM connectors, APIs |
| Admin | Per-user seats, roles, business-hours rules |
| Support | Phone/chat support as a selling point |

## 3. User flow

1. Pick a plan on the site (or request a demo).
2. Choose number type; add toll-free as a paid add-on.
3. Buy **seats per user**; each user gets a softphone/agent login.
4. Configure IVR and routing.
5. Add AI agents as an additional conversation.

The seat model is the structural difference from us: **the office pays per
person, whether or not that person makes calls.**

## 4. Pricing

| Item | Published | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | **$19.99 / user / month** | from Capterra |
| Higher tier | **$29.99 / user / month** | from Capterra |
| Toll-free number | **+$4.99 / month** | add-on |
| AI voice agent per minute | not published on a price page we could read | their blog estimates ₹0.80–3.00/min for AI voice bots in India |

Two things to be careful about when quoting this:
- The **$19.99–29.99 is per user per month**, not per minute. For a 3-person
  college office that is ~$60–90/month *before* any calling.
- The **₹0.80–3.00/min** figure is from **Acefone's own blog** describing the
  Indian market range, not a published Acefone AI rate. Treat it as their
  marketing, not their price list.

## 5. The words they use

Keyword vocabulary: cloud phone system, virtual number, toll-free, IVR, call
centre, small business, easy setup, support, per user, unlimited calling plans,
AI voice agents, CRM integration.

Tone: SMB-friendly, support-led ("we answer the phone"), feature checklist.
Value framed as **per-seat**, not per-outcome.

## 6. Where they are stronger

- **Fast SMB sales and support** — a small business can talk to a human quickly.
- **Number provisioning in multiple countries.**
- **All-in-one phone system** — if the buyer needs a business phone system, not
  just campaigns, Acefone is the more complete purchase.
- **Bundled support** is a real differentiator against developer platforms.

## 7. Gaps we attack

1. **Seat pricing.** A college with two office staff pays for two seats forever.
   We charge only for minutes actually spoken.
2. **The AI minute is not the product.** Their console is a phone system; the AI
   agent is an add-on to a seat-based plan. Our whole product is the campaign.
3. **No structured extraction.** Same gap as the rest of the market: transcript
   and recording, not columns.
4. **No vertical templates** for attendance/fees/admissions.
5. **Cost predictability.** Seat + add-ons + usage is hard to forecast; ₹3/min
   with a visible wallet is not.
6. **Automation of the "boring" part** — roster import, retries, escalation,
   report — is absent.

## 8. Demo moves / how we win side-by-side

- Do the **seats math** on the whiteboard: 2 staff × $29.99 × 12 = ~$720/year
  before one call. Then show our Starter ₹2,990/1,000 min.
- Show a **campaign end-to-end**; ask them to show the same in a phone-system
  console.
- Show the **report and the escalation list**, which no seat-based phone system
  produces.
- Concede general phone-system needs; position as the **outbound campaign layer**
  if the buyer already has desk phones.
- Before any competitive deal, re-check their real AI rate by phone — their site
  could not be read by automation and the blog number is not a price list.
