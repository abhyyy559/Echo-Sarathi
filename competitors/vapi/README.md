# Vapi — competitor profile

## Scope and evidence confidence
- Evidence date: 2026-10-28; supplied main-agent Browser Use observations only.
- Homepage and pricing page were opened; no additional research was performed for this rewrite.
- Review covered public text/DOM content and structure, not a working dashboard session.
- Screenshots and clicks failed with `NATIVE_BROWSER_VIEWPORT_UNAVAILABLE` (0x0 unattached surface).
- No signup, demo execution, paid action, call, listening test, or logged-in use occurred.
- Confidence is high in the recorded wording and published plan figures, not implementation quality.
- Marketing capabilities and enterprise commitments were not independently validated.

## Positioning and exact headline
- Exact headline: **“Voice agents for builders”**.
- Supporting line: **“Your agents, your rules.”**
- Positioning interpretation: a configurable voice-agent platform for builders and operating teams.
- The public story spans building through monitoring; it is not evidence of a runtime-only product.

## Publicly advertised features
- Tools and integrations for connecting agents to external systems.
- Observability for monitoring agent operation.
- Testing and simulation capabilities.
- SSO, OAuth and role-based access control (RBAC).
- Enterprise service-level agreements and reserved capacity.
- A build/configure/test/deploy sequence followed by monitoring and improvement.
- Homepage demo choices cover qualification, support and scheduling.
- Feature availability by plan needs confirmation; marketing coverage is not proof of a tested entitlement.

## Public acquisition flow versus gated dashboard
- The homepage describes configuring and testing agents before deployment.
- The public demo surface presented qualification/support/scheduling choices.
- The browser showed a microphone-blocked state; no demo was exercised or heard.
- Trial credits and a phone-number allowance are advertised in the entry package.
- Signup requirements, provisioning, checkout and time to first call were not tested.
- No logged-in campaign builder, extraction configuration or dashboard controls were inspected.
- Campaign and extraction support were not checked sufficiently to claim either is absent.

## Pricing and inclusions
| Success package | Package charge | Concurrent calls | Raw retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Success Package | $0 | 4 | 14 days |
| Core | $29/month | 10 | 30 days |
| Pro | 10% of hosting fee, $999/month minimum | 30 | Not recorded in supplied evidence |
| Premier | Custom | Not recorded in supplied evidence | Not recorded in supplied evidence |

- Voice hosting is $0.05/min, plus model passthrough and provider transport charges.
- The $0 No Success Package is not free voice usage.
- That entry package advertises $5 trial credit and one phone number.
- Additional concurrency is advertised at $10 per line per month.
- Core includes 10 concurrent calls, correcting the prior profile's figure of four.
- Pro is a percentage-based charge with a minimum, not simply a flat $999 plan.
- The default calculator showed $82–129 for 1,000 minutes with selected components.
- That calculator output is configuration-specific, not an India telephony or all-inclusive quote.
- No currency conversion, tax inclusion, GST-invoice policy or purchase-order support was verified.

## Public frontend-stack signals
- `seasonSans` was observed as the public font signal.
- No server stack was verified; the supplied evidence does not establish a frontend framework.
- Do not infer account-app architecture from the public site's typography.
- Screenshot appearance, pointer behavior and perceived interaction speed were not evaluated.

## UX lessons and hypotheses, not proven gaps
- Lesson: explain the full build-to-improve lifecycle rather than only initial agent creation.
- Lesson: task-based demo choices can communicate intended use cases before account creation.
- Hypothesis: a preflight microphone message could help users distinguish permissions from product failure.
- Hypothesis: separating hosting, models, transport and package charges could improve quote comprehension.
- Hypothesis: an institution-specific workflow example may help a non-developer evaluate fit.
- These are design ideas to validate, not findings that Vapi lacks workflows or usable onboarding.
- Consent, templates, tenant branding, INR billing and procurement support remain unverified here.
- No audio quality, latency, cost advantage or time-to-value comparison was measured.

## Corrections from the prior profile
- Replaced the older developer headline with the observed builder headline.
- Corrected Core concurrency, Pro charging structure and the distinction between packages and usage.
- Removed blanket claims that campaigns require a custom backend or extraction is unavailable.
- Removed unsupported claims about absent Indian consent/compliance, GST invoices or purchase orders.
- Removed the invented account walkthrough and claims about engineering effort or launch delays.
- Removed exchange-rate anchors, assumed all-in savings and unsupported provider-capacity comparisons.
