# Bland AI — competitor profile

Researched 2026-10-28 from bland.ai pricing. USD at ~₹88.

**One-liner:** Self-serve AI phone calls at a flat per-minute rate.
**Type:** Global AI-native voice calling platform.
**Threat level:** **Low in India.** Flat pricing is attractive on paper until
you convert it: ₹10.6–12.3/min.

---

## 1. What they sell

AI agents that place and answer phone calls. Bland's pitch is simplicity: one
flat per-minute price with the models **included**, so the buyer does not have to
assemble an STT/LLM/TTS stack. Positioned for sales, support and operations
teams that want calls running without a platform-engineering project.

## 2. Feature set

| Area | What they ship |
|---|---|
| Agents | Prompt/pathway-based conversation design, voice selection |
| Telephony | Outbound calling, inbound numbers |
| Models | **Bundled** — no separate token billing |
| Pathways | Structured conversation paths (branching dialogue) |
| Tools | Webhooks, transfers, custom actions |
| Analytics | Call logs, recordings, transcripts |
| Scale | Enterprise tier for volume and custom needs |

## 3. User flow

1. Sign up, get a number.
2. Write the agent or build a **pathway** (branching tree).
3. Test a call.
4. Launch outbound calls or point a number at the agent for inbound.
5. Read transcripts; integrate via webhooks.

Straightforward self-serve. No roster-driven campaign product with verification
and reporting, and no India-specific compliance layer.

## 4. Pricing

| Plan | Published | ≈ ₹/min |
|---|---|---|
| Start | **$0.14 / min** | **12.32** |
| Build | **$0.12 / min** | **10.56** |
| Enterprise | custom | — |
| Models | included, **"no token charges"** | — |

Flat and predictable, which is genuinely appealing. But **₹10.5–12.3/min is
3.5–4× our price** for a call of the same length. For a 500-student attendance
campaign at 45 seconds a call, the gap is roughly ₹3,700 vs ₹1,125.

## 5. The words they use

Keywords: AI phone calls, agents, pathways, no token charges, flat pricing,
self-serve, scale, enterprise, outbound, inbound, call center.

Tone: simple, confident, "flat price, no surprises". US-centric; no Indic
languages, DLT, GST or local-number story in the primary pitch.

## 6. Where they are stronger

- **Flat pricing** is easier to budget than component passthrough.
- **Simplicity of onboarding** — very little configuration to make a first call.
- **Pathways** give non-technical users a structured dialogue editor.
- **US telephony** and enterprise tier for volume buyers.
- **No model cost anxiety** — a real objection we hear from buyers burned by
  token bills.

## 7. Gaps we attack

1. **₹10.56 is 3.5× ₹3.00.** Price alone disqualifies them for Indian
   institutions.
2. **No Indic language or accent quality story.** Telugu conversations are our
   proof point.
3. **No India compliance** — DLT, calling hours, consent, opt-out.
4. **No roster → report workflow.** Pathways help design a conversation, not run
   a 500-student campaign with verification and escalation.
5. **USD billing** — no GST invoice, no PO.
6. **Pathways are rigid** relative to an LLM-led extraction conversation; a
   parent who answers off-script still needs the reason captured.

## 8. Demo moves / how we win side-by-side

- Convert the flat rate to rupees **per campaign**, not per minute — it is more
  persuasive at scale.
- Play a **Telugu call**; ask what it sounds like on their default voices.
- Show the **report** and the escalation queue.
- Borrow their best idea: our pricing is *also* flat and predictable, we just do
  it at a third of the price with everything included.
