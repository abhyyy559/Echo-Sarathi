# Knowlarity — competitor profile

Researched 2026-10-28 from public pricing extracts. Knowlarity is part of
**Gupshup**. Pricing below is **telephony only** — AI agent minutes are a
separate product.

**One-liner:** Cloud telephony and communication APIs for Indian businesses.
**Type:** Legacy Indian CPaaS (Gupshup company).
**Threat level:** **Low-medium.** Cheap telephony, but not a voice-AI campaign
product; buyers comparing AI agents will not shortlist it head-to-head.

---

## 1. What they sell

Virtual numbers, cloud telephony, IVR, call tracking, and communication APIs
(voice, SMS, WhatsApp via Gupshup). Historically strong in **inbound customer
support numbers** and **outbound telemarketing telephony** for SMBs.

## 2. Feature set

| Area | What they ship |
|---|---|
| Numbers | Virtual numbers, toll-free, DID |
| Telephony | Outbound calling, inbound IVR, call routing, call recording |
| IVR | Menu-based IVR flows, business-hours routing |
| Analytics | Call reports, recordings, basic dashboards |
| API | Voice/SMS APIs, CRM webhooks |
| AI | AI calling/voice bot offerings exist as a separate line — not covered by the telephony rate |

## 3. User flow

1. Enquire → sales callback (SMB-friendly, faster than enterprise vendors).
2. Pick a number type and a plan.
3. KYC and number activation.
4. Configure IVR/routing in a web console.
5. Add AI calling as a separate discussion.

Not fully self-serve; expect a sales conversation and number KYC.

## 4. Pricing

| Item | Published | ≈ ₹/min |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound calls | **70 paise / 30 s** | **1.40** |
| Toll-free incoming | **₹1.05 / min** | 1.05 |
| AI voice agent | not bundled | separate quote |

The trap for a buyer: **₹1.40 is the phone line, not the agent.** Add STT, LLM,
TTS and orchestration and the real cost lands close to ours or above it, with
none of the campaign workflow. This is the single most misread price in the
Indian market and a good slide for us.

## 5. The words they use

Keyword vocabulary: cloud telephony, virtual number, toll-free, IVR, call
tracking, missed call, lead management, customer support, SMB, Gupshup,
WhatsApp business.

Tone: SMB utility. Cheap, practical, "get a business number". Almost no
language about agents, extraction or outcomes.

## 6. Where they are stronger

- **Number inventory and telephony maturity** at low cost.
- **SMB sales motion** — they will call you back quickly and close small deals.
- **Gupshup distribution** — WhatsApp/SMS cross-sell into the same account.
- **₹1.40/min telephony** is genuinely cheap as a *line*.

## 7. Gaps we attack

1. **It is not an AI agent.** No LLM conversation, no extraction, no structured
   report. The comparison is apples to a different fruit — say so plainly.
2. **No campaign workflow** for a roster: no verification step, no per-student
   outcome, no escalation rules.
3. **No vernacular conversation quality** claim we cannot beat in Telugu.
4. **IVR is menu-driven**, not conversational — a parent who says "fever" gets a
   keypad menu, not an answer.
5. **True cost is hidden** behind three separate line items (telephony + AI +
   platform) — we quote one all-in number.

## 8. Demo moves / how we win side-by-side

- Put the **₹1.40 telephony rate** next to the **all-in ₹3.00** and explain what
  the extra ₹1.60 buys: STT, LLM, TTS, extraction, report. Then show what a
  DIY stack on top of ₹1.40 actually costs.
- Play a **Telugu parent conversation** against an IVR menu.
- Show the **report**; there is no equivalent artifact in their product.
- Do not attack their telephony — if anything, note we can sit on top of Indian
  lines (Vobiz today) and are not trying to be a phone company.
