# Ozonetel — competitor profile

Researched 2026-10-28. Their site was **unreachable from this machine** during
research (connection timeouts; one redirect resolved to a private IP and was not
followed). Everything below is from public descriptions and is marked
accordingly. **Pricing is not public — quote only.**

**One-liner:** Cloud contact centre for Indian enterprises, now with AI agents.
**Type:** Legacy Indian cloud contact-centre / CPaaS vendor.
**Threat level:** **Medium.** Strong in BPO/collections, invisible to a college
principal who wants to launch this week.

---

## 1. What they sell

A cloud contact-centre platform: inbound and outbound voice, IVR, dialer,
agent desktop, supervisor tooling, analytics, and workforce management — with
AI voice agents and AI-assisted agent tooling layered on. Their historical
strength is the **outbound dialer** for sales and collections operations.

## 2. Feature set

| Area | What they ship (public descriptions) |
|---|---|
| Contact centre | Inbound queues, IVR, skills routing, agent desktop |
| Outbound | Predictive/progressive dialer, campaign management, retries |
| AI | AI voice agents, AI copilots/summaries for human agents |
| Supervisor | Live monitoring, barge-in, recordings, quality scoring |
| Integrations | CRM connectors, APIs, webhooks |
| Deployment | Cloud, multi-tenant, India-hosted options |
| Analytics | Wallboards, campaign and agent reporting |

**Confidence note:** feature list is assembled from public marketing and review
sites, not a first-hand walkthrough — the site blocked automated access.

## 3. User flow (as understood)

1. Inquiry via site or sales contact → demo.
2. Solution design around seat count, dialer minutes and channels.
3. Commercial proposal (quote), contract, onboarding.
4. Campaigns and IVR flows configured with implementation support.
5. Ongoing account management.

Self-serve signup and public per-minute AI pricing are **not part of the flow**.

## 4. Pricing

| Item | Published? |

For a price-led sale, this vendor cannot be compared — and that is an advantage

## 5. The words they use

Keyword vocabulary: cloud contact centre, outbound dialer, predictive dialer,
collections, BPO, customer experience, agent desktop, supervisor, IVR,
workforce management, AI agents, India-hosted, enterprise-grade.

Tone: operations-first, enterprise procurement language. The buyer they write
for is a contact-centre head, not a department that makes 40 calls a day.

## 6. Where they are stronger

- **Dialer maturity** — pacing, retries, list management refined over years of
  collections/BPO operations.
- **Supervisor tooling** — live monitoring, barge-in, quality scoring.
- **Enterprise footprint** in BPO, BFSI and telecom operations.
- **Human + AI hybrid** — human agents and AI agents in one queue; we have no
  human-agent layer at all.
- **Workforce management** — scheduling and adherence, entirely outside our scope.

## 7. Gaps we attack

1. **No public price.** We publish ₹3/min and show the balance.
2. **No vertical product.** They sell a contact-centre platform; a college needs
   an attendance campaign, not a dialer to configure.
3. **Enterprise-only deal shape.** Minimums and implementation cycles exclude
   small institutions; our Starter plan is ₹2,990.
4. **AI is an add-on to a legacy console**, so the UX is built around human
   agents. Our flow is built around a campaign with no human in the loop.
5. **No structured extraction report** targeted at a non-technical buyer.
6. **Opaque capacity.** Concurrency is a commercial term, not a visible gauge.

## 8. Demo moves / how we win side-by-side

- If a buyer is comparing quotes, ask what the **per-call cost** is; most cannot
  answer from Ozonetel's proposal without a spreadsheet.
- Show the **whole campaign in one screen** — roster, dialer, outcome, report —
  against a console designed for 50 human agents.
- Win the **small institution** outright; do not try to displace them inside a
  BPO.
- Re-verify their current pricing before any competitive deal — the site was not
  readable during this research.
