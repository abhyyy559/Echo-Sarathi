# Retell AI — competitor profile

## Scope and evidence confidence
- Evidence date: 2026-10-28; supplied main-agent Browser Use observations only.
- The homepage was fully inspected through public text/DOM; no new research was performed here.
- The pricing-page link was observed but the pricing page was not opened in this pass.
- Screenshots and clicks failed with `NATIVE_BROWSER_VIEWPORT_UNAVAILABLE` (0x0 unattached surface).
- No signup, demo submission, paid action, call, listening test, or logged-in use occurred.
- Confidence is high in the recorded homepage claims; runtime behavior remains untested.
- Enterprise access, privacy and on-premises claims are vendor statements, not independent assurance.

## Positioning and exact headline
- Exact headline: **“Meet your AI call center from the future.”**
- Positioning interpretation: a voice and contact-center platform with operational workflow coverage.
- Its public pitch extends beyond agent infrastructure to deployment, quality assurance and analytics.
- Treat it as a direct workflow comparator, not merely a transcript-producing runtime.

## Publicly advertised features
- No-code studio organized around Build, Test, Deploy, QA and Analytics.
- Visual call flows with functions, DTMF, SMS and transfers.
- Campaign CSV upload, scheduling, pacing, retries and voicemail handling.
- Live listening and human takeover are advertised capabilities; neither was used in this review.
- CRM data entering the agent workflow and outcomes returning to the CRM.
- Post-call analysis, which contradicts a transcripts-only characterization.
- Chat, voice, SMS and API surfaces.
- Enterprise access controls, privacy and on-premises options are publicly claimed.
- This list records marketing coverage, not tested availability across every plan.

## Public acquisition flow versus gated dashboard
- The homepage presents the Build -> Test -> Deploy -> QA -> Analytics lifecycle.
- A public demo form requests a name and phone number.
- No personal information was submitted and no demonstration call was requested.
- The no-code studio and visual flows were described publicly, not explored after login.
- Account creation, number provisioning, checkout and setup duration remain unknown.
- Campaign imports, retry behavior, analytics and human takeover were not exercised.
- Public workflow descriptions support feature recognition, not claims about ease or reliability.

## UX lessons and hypotheses, not proven gaps
- Lesson: make the operational lifecycle as visible as agent construction.
- Lesson: explicitly connect CRM context, call behavior and returned outcomes.
- Lesson: campaign pacing, retries and human intervention are meaningful public evaluation topics.
- Hypothesis: a worked institution-specific example could clarify fit within an already broad workflow.
- Hypothesis: showing configuration assumptions alongside a quote could make the range easier to use.
- Neither hypothesis implies that Retell lacks templates, extraction or reporting capabilities.
- Consent, tenant branding and local procurement support remain unverified, not established omissions.
- No comparative voice quality, latency, usability or campaign outcome was measured.

## Corrections from the prior profile
- Explicitly reject the false “transcripts only/no workflow” framing.
- Public evidence includes CSV campaigns, retries, CRM outcomes and post-call analysis.
- Removed claims that roster import, workflow automation or vertical templates are absent.
- Removed unsupported Indian compliance, GST/PO and card-only assertions.
- Removed an imagined dashboard walkthrough, quality rankings and assumed pricing superiority.
- Reattributed the rate, trial and concurrency figures to the homepage FAQ actually reviewed.
