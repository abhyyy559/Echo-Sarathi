# ElevenLabs — competitor profile

Researched 2026-10-28 from elevenlabs.io pricing. USD at ~₹88.
Only their **TTS rate** is a clean published per-minute number; the agent minute
is a composition of TTS + LLM + platform, so treat the end-to-end figure as an
estimate, not a price list.

**One-liner:** The best-known AI voice company, with a conversational agents
platform on top.
**Type:** Voice-first AI platform (TTS heritage → agents).
**Threat level:** **Low for our buyer, high for voice quality comparisons.**
They set the quality bar, not the campaign price.

---

## 1. What they sell

Two things that matter here:
1. **Text-to-speech** — the quality benchmark for cloned and synthetic voices.
2. **Agents platform** — conversational voice agents (prompt, tools, knowledge
   base, phone numbers) built on that voice stack.

They are the vendor other vendors buy their voices from, which makes them both a
competitor and a potential supplier to us.

## 2. Feature set

| Area | What they ship |
|---|---|
| TTS | Large voice library, instant + professional voice cloning |
| Agents | Prompt/knowledge-base-driven conversational agents |
| Telephony | Phone number integrations for inbound/outbound agents |
| Tools | Webhooks, client tools, integrations |
| Multilingual | Broad language list including Indian languages |
| Voice design | Generate/modify voices by description |
| Observability | Conversation history, transcripts, analytics on paid tiers |
| Studio | Long-form dubbing and narration tooling |

## 3. User flow

1. Sign up → pick or clone a voice.
2. Create an agent → choose voice, model, prompt, attach knowledge.
3. Test in browser or on a number.
4. Launch calls (inbound first-class; outbound via integrations).
5. Review transcripts and usage; upgrade tier as characters/minutes grow.

The buyer is usually a product or content team, not an institution running a
roster.

## 4. Pricing

| Item | Published | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TTS | **"as low as 5¢ / minute"** | at volume; higher on low tiers |
| Business plan | **10 voice clones, 10 seats** | seat-based team plan |
| Agents | TTS + LLM + platform composed | **no single published all-in rate** we could verify |
| Billing | subscription + usage in USD | card only |

The honest read: **5¢/min ≈ ₹4.40 is the voice line alone**, before LLM,
telephony and platform. An all-in ElevenLabs agent is comfortably above our
₹3.00, and the plan structure is seat + subscription, which is the wrong shape
for a two-person college office.

Mark any end-to-end ElevenLabs number in a deck as an **estimate** — do not
present it as a published rate.

## 5. The words they use

Keywords: realistic AI voice, voice cloning, text to speech, conversational AI,
agents, multilingual, studio, dubbing, expressive, low latency, enterprise.

Tone: craft and quality. Voice samples and demos carry the sale. Almost no
vertical or compliance language, and no Indian procurement story.

## 6. Where they are stronger

- **Voice quality** — the benchmark. If a buyer says our Telugu sounds robotic,
  ElevenLabs is what they are comparing against.
- **Voice cloning** per brand or per speaker.
- **Multilingual breadth** and voice design tooling.
- **Brand recognition** — the name buyers have already heard.
- **Content tooling** (studio, dubbing) that we do not touch.

## 7. Gaps we attack

1. **They sell voices and a builder; we sell a finished campaign.** No roster,
   no verification, no escalation, no institutional report.
2. **Price shape.** Subscription + seats + usage vs ₹3/min with a visible wallet.
3. **No India compliance or GST/PO path.**
4. **No vertical templates** for attendance, fees or admissions.
5. **No campaign console** with concurrency against a plan cap.
6. **No outcome columns** a principal can read; transcripts at best.

## 8. Demo moves / how we win side-by-side

- **Do not fight on voice quality.** If the buyer prefers their voice, that is a
  legitimate preference — note that we can evaluate their TTS as a supplier and
  win on the workflow and price.
- Compare **per-campaign cost** including subscription and seats.
- Show the **report**, the escalation list and the concurrency gauge.
- Use the voice-quality conversation to open a **provider negotiation**: their
  TTS line is the largest single component of our ₹2.30 cost floor.
