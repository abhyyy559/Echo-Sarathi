# ElevenLabs Agents — competitor profile

## Scope and evidence confidence
- Evidence date: 2026-10-28; supplied main-agent Browser Use observations only.
- The Agents landing page and Agents pricing page were both opened; no new research was added.
- Scope is the Agents product, not a substitution of text-to-speech pricing for agent pricing.
- Public text/DOM content and structure were reviewed; no interactive or visual evaluation succeeded.
- Screenshots and clicks failed with `NATIVE_BROWSER_VIEWPORT_UNAVAILABLE` (0x0 unattached surface).
- No signup, demo execution, paid action, call, listening test, or logged-in use occurred.
- Confidence is high in recorded public offers; delivery, audio quality and residency were not audited.

## Positioning and exact headline
- Exact headline: **“Conversational agents that feel human”**.
- Positioning interpretation: a conversational-agent product spanning voice and messaging channels.
- The headline is a vendor promise, not a finding about human likeness or voice superiority.
- Compare the Agents offering on its own terms rather than treating it as a TTS-only supplier.

## Publicly advertised features
- 90+ languages are claimed; supported-language quality was not tested.
- Voice, chat, email, WhatsApp and SMS are publicly presented channels.
- Prompt configuration, knowledge bases, workflows and tools.
- Analytics, guardrails and simulations.
- Templates and a clone-agent capability.
- Integrations with other services.
- Enterprise India data residency is offered; this is not a default for every customer or plan.
- Feature coverage is advertised, not verified through a logged-in account.

## Public acquisition flow versus gated dashboard
- The landing page introduces agent capabilities and the separate Agents pricing page packages access.
- Free and paid plans provide public entry choices; templates and clone-agent are advertised.
- No account was created, agent cloned or template configured.
- No voice selection, knowledge upload, channel connection or test conversation was performed.
- Checkout, upgrade behavior, account prerequisites and time to first usable agent remain unknown.
- Public feature descriptions do not establish the exact sequence or usability of the gated builder.

## Public frontend-stack signals
- Next.js and Inter were publicly observed.
- These signals concern the public frontend, not a complete authenticated-app or server architecture.
- No visual-polish ranking, pointer behavior or rendering-performance assessment was possible.

## UX lessons and hypotheses, not proven gaps
- Lesson: separate the Agents product and its allowances from a broader audio-product catalogue.
- Lesson: expose workflows, simulations and guardrails alongside the voice proposition.
- Hypothesis: a plan comparison that separates included minutes, overage, LLM and carrier costs could help budgeting.
- Hypothesis: clearly labeling residency eligibility could help buyers distinguish options from defaults.
- Hypothesis: an institution-specific template example could clarify fit without implying templates are missing.
- Extraction, consent specifics, tenant branding and local procurement support were not established here.
- No first-hand audio comparison supports claims that this vendor is better or worse than another.
