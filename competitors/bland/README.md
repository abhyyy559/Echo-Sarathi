# Bland AI — competitor profile

## Scope and evidence confidence
- Evidence date: 2026-10-28; supplied main-agent Browser Use observations only.
- Homepage and pricing page were opened; this rewrite performs no new web research.
- Review covered public text/DOM content and structure, not interactive product use.
- Screenshots and clicks failed with `NATIVE_BROWSER_VIEWPORT_UNAVAILABLE` (0x0 unattached surface).
- No signup, demo execution, paid action, call, listening test, or logged-in use occurred.
- Confidence is high in the recorded copy and plan figures, with a telephony-inclusion ambiguity below.
- Language coverage and the enterprise own-model stack are vendor claims, not independent findings.

## Positioning and exact headline
- Exact headline: **“Voice AI for high-stakes calls”**.
- Positioning interpretation: voice automation emphasizing operational control and enterprise use.
- The Build / Monitor / Improve story extends beyond a simple flat-price calling pitch.
- No regional suitability, voice quality or market-threat ranking was measured.

## Publicly advertised features
- Pathways for defining conversations.
- Tool calls and integrations.
- Safe testing and versioning.
- A Build -> Monitor -> Improve lifecycle.
- 40+ languages are claimed; exact language quality was not evaluated.
- An enterprise own-model stack is claimed, without independent architecture verification.
- Enterprise packaging advertises richer features and custom commercial terms.
- These statements do not establish which features are available on every self-serve plan.

## Public acquisition flow versus gated dashboard
- The public site explains building, monitoring and improving agents.
- Start and Build provide visible entry-plan choices, alongside a custom enterprise option.
- The Start trial advertises two credits and an inbound number.
- No trial was activated and no number was provisioned.
- Signup, account configuration, pathway editing and payment steps were not traversed.
- Monitoring screens, tool behavior and deployment workflows remain untested.
- The public sequence is not evidence of frictionless onboarding or a completed dashboard walkthrough.

## Pricing and inclusions
| Plan | Agent rate | Platform charge | Concurrent calls | Daily call allowance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | $0.14/min | $0 | 10 | 100 calls/day |
| Build | $0.12/min | $299/month | 50 | 2,000 calls/day |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Not recorded | Not recorded |

- LLM, speech-to-text and text-to-speech are included in the advertised agent rates.
- The pricing FAQ says telephony is separate, through an own-carrier arrangement or passthrough.
- A homepage sentence describes telephony as bundled, contradicting that pricing FAQ.
- Prefer the pricing FAQ for quote assumptions and explicitly confirm the conflicting inclusion before purchase.
- Do not represent either public rate as an unqualified all-in telephony price.
- Transfer rates are $0.05/min for Start and $0.04/min for Build.
- Start advertises two trial credits plus an inbound number; no usable trial duration was inferred.
- Build's monthly platform fee must accompany its lower per-minute rate in comparisons.
- The recorded concurrency and daily allowances are plan-specific, not universal limits.
- Taxes, INR settlement, GST invoices and purchase-order support were not verified.

## Public frontend-stack signals
- Soehne was observed as the public font.

- The enterprise own-model marketing claim is not a frontend-stack observation.
- No screenshot review, pointer interaction or frontend performance benchmark was completed.

## UX lessons and hypotheses, not proven gaps
- Lesson: describe testing and versioning alongside conversation-building features.
- Lesson: connect the initial build to monitoring and subsequent improvement.
- Hypothesis: colocating the platform fee, carrier charges and transfer rate could improve budget clarity.
- Hypothesis: consistent telephony-inclusion wording could prevent misunderstandings at quote time.
- Hypothesis: concrete outcome examples may help buyers evaluate a high-stakes use case.
- These are design hypotheses, not proof that outcomes, extraction or campaign workflows are absent.
- Consent, templates, tenant branding and Indian procurement support remain unverified here.
- No language-quality ranking, pathway-rigidity test or onboarding-speed comparison was performed.
